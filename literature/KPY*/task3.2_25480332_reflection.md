# My Reflection on Writing

## Writing Challenges and Difficulties

**What aspects of academic writing do you find most challenging?**
The most challenging aspect is writing a revised draft. If the draft has no major problems in terms of structure and basic content, it will greatly help with the subsequent writing. However, if the draft has significant problems in terms of structure and basic content, it will have a negative impact on the subsequent writing.

**What specific difficulties do you face when writing Introduction/Literature Review?**
Challenge: Integrating existing research literature according to one's own categorization.

**What do you struggle with most?**
How to write a suitable first draft

## My Writing Process

**How do you approach writing Introduction and Literature Review?**
Based on the provided outline, present the core content in short sentences, then connect them together to create the complete content. Multiple revisions will be made during the writing process.

**What steps do you take?**
Drafting an outline, connecting the content, and writing the detailed text.
